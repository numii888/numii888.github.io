Your logo is often the first thing people see about your brand. Here are 5 signs it's time for a redesign...

## 1. It looks outdated
If your logo hasn't changed in 5+ years, chances are it feels dated compared to modern brands.

## 2. It doesn't work on mobile
Modern logos need to look sharp on tiny screens too — a cluttered or overly detailed logo often becomes unreadable at small sizes.

## 3. It doesn't reflect who you are today
Businesses evolve. If your services, values, or target audience have changed, your logo should reflect that growth too.

## 4. It looks like everyone else's
If your logo blends in with competitors instead of standing out, it's not doing its job of building brand recognition.

## 5. You're embarrassed to share it
If you hesitate before putting your logo on a business card, website, or social media — that's the clearest sign of all.
