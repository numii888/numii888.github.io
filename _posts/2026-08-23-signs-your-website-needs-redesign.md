Most businesses don't realize their website is losing them customers — it just looks fine, so no one questions it. But an outdated or poorly built site can be turning visitors away before they ever reach out. Here are 7 warning signs worth checking today.

## 1. It takes more than 3 seconds to load

Visitors don't wait. Studies consistently show that a slow-loading site causes a sharp jump in people leaving before the page even finishes loading — meaning they never see what you offer.

## 2. It doesn't look right on mobile

Most traffic today comes from phones. If text is cut off, buttons are hard to tap, or images overflow the screen, visitors bounce immediately — and Google notices too, ranking mobile-unfriendly sites lower.

## 3. Your last update was years ago

An outdated design signals an outdated business, fairly or not. Visitors judge credibility fast — a stale-looking site quietly damages trust before a single word is read.

## 4. There's no clear call-to-action

If a visitor doesn't immediately know what to do next — "Contact Us," "Order Now," "Get a Quote" — they'll leave without acting, even if they were interested.

## 5. Your competitors' sites look more professional

If a potential customer compares your site to a competitor's and yours feels dated in comparison, that comparison alone can cost you the sale.

## 6. It's hard to find basic information

Contact details, services, and pricing should be easy to find within seconds. A confusing layout frustrates visitors and pushes them toward a competitor instead.

## 7. It isn't showing up in search results

If your site isn't optimized for search engines, potential customers searching for exactly what you offer may never find you at all.

## The fix

A redesign doesn't have to mean starting from zero — often it means fixing speed, mobile responsiveness, and clarity so your site works as hard as your business does.
