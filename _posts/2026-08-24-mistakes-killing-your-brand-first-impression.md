You never get a second chance at a first impression — and for most businesses, that first impression happens online, in about 3 seconds. Here are 6 quiet mistakes that push potential customers away before they even realize it.

## 1. Inconsistent branding across platforms

If your logo, colors, or tone look different on your website versus your Instagram versus your business card, it signals disorganization — even if each piece looks good on its own.

## 2. A cluttered, busy design

More elements don't mean more value. Too many colors, fonts, or competing visuals overwhelm visitors and bury the one thing that actually matters: your message.

## 3. Generic stock photography

Overused stock images make a brand feel interchangeable with a thousand others. Custom visuals — even simple ones — build recognition that stock photos never will.

## 4. Unclear value proposition

If a visitor can't tell what you do and why it matters within seconds of landing on your page, they'll leave before finding out. Clarity beats cleverness every time.

## 5. Poor typography choices

Hard-to-read fonts, inconsistent sizing, or too many typefaces at once quietly damage credibility — readers associate messy typography with a messy business.

## 6. No visible proof of trust

Missing testimonials, reviews, or case studies leaves visitors wondering if anyone else has actually used your service. Social proof isn't optional — it's often the deciding factor.

## The fix

None of these require a full rebrand — usually just focused adjustments to bring consistency, clarity, and trust signals into alignment.
