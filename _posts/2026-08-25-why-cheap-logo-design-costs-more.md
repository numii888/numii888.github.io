A cheap logo feels like a smart way to save money early on — but for most businesses, it ends up being one of the most expensive shortcuts they take. Here's why the real cost shows up later, not upfront.

## Cheap logos are rarely original

Many budget logo services rely on templates or slight variations of existing designs. If your logo closely resembles another business's, it weakens your brand identity and can even create legal risk down the line.

## They don't scale well

A design that looks fine at one size often breaks apart at another — pixelated on a billboard, unreadable as a tiny app icon, or messy when printed. Without proper vector source files, every future use becomes a problem to fix.

## Rebranding costs far more than the logo itself

When a cheap logo eventually gets replaced, the cost isn't just a new design — it's updating your website, packaging, social media, business cards, signage, and every place your old logo lived. That total cost is almost always higher than investing properly the first time.

## First impressions are hard to undo

Customers form an opinion about your business within seconds of seeing your branding. A generic or poorly designed logo can quietly signal "low quality" — even if your actual product or service is excellent.

## What a proper investment actually buys you

A well-designed logo isn't just a picture — it's built around your brand's positioning, tested for versatility, and delivered with the files you'll actually need for years of use across every platform.

## The real math

A $10 logo that gets replaced within a year, plus the cost of redesigning everything around it, almost always ends up more expensive than paying for it done right the first time.
