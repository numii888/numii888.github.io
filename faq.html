Answers to the questions clients ask most before starting a project with DesignedDev Studio.

How long does a logo design project take?

Most logo projects are delivered within 2 days, from the first concept to final vector files.

What file formats will I receive?

Logo projects include vector source files (AI/SVG) so your logo scales cleanly from a favicon to a billboard, along with common formats like PNG for everyday use.

How much does a logo cost?

The Logo Pack starts at $59.99 (currently 35% off the regular $92 price) and includes 3 custom concept blueprints plus vector source files.

Do you offer UI/UX design services?

Yes — the UI/UX Pack includes high-fidelity Figma screens, user flow mapping, and dev-ready handoff, starting at $149.99 (35% off the regular $231 price).

Can you build a full website too?

Yes — the Web Dev Pack includes a fully responsive, SEO-optimized website build, starting at $249.99 (35% off the regular $385 price).

How many revisions are included?

Logo Pack and UI/UX Pack each include 3 free revisions. The Web Dev Pack includes 2 free revisions. This gives us room to refine the design until it fits your brand.

How experienced is DesignedDev Studio?

DesignedDev Studio is led by Muhammad Numan, with over 3 years of hands-on experience in logo design, UI/UX, and web development.

How do I pay for my order?

Once you reach out and confirm your package, payment details are shared and discussed directly in chat — so we can walk you through the process personally.

What payment methods do you accept?

We offer two secure payment options: Tap Tap Send, which supports over 53 currencies for easy local-currency payments, and Binance (Cryptocurrency), accepted globally and covering virtually any currency worldwide. Account details for payment are shared directly with you once your order is booked.

How can I get the best results from my project?

The more detail you share about your brand and requirements upfront, the better the outcome. We ask clients to provide their full requirements early on so the design is built around your business from the start, not adjusted after the fact.

How do I get started?

Reach out with a short description of your project through WhatsApp, email, or Telegram — whichever is easiest for you — and we'll respond with next steps and pricing.
